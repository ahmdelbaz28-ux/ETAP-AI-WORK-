    IEEE 80-2013 Substation Grounding Grid Safety Charter & PE Risk Acceptance Policy

    Document ID: SAFETY-EARTH-001
    Classification: HIGH RISK / HIGH-VOLTAGE SUBSTATION LIFE-SAFETY & GROUND FAULT ENVELOPE
    Standards Compliance: IEEE 80-2013, IEEE 81-2012, NFPA 70E-2024, IEC 61936-1:2021
    Date: 2026-10-12
    Authority: Platform Safety Board / Licensed Professional Engineering (PE) Committee

    1. Executive Safety Summary

    The earth_grid capability (StudyType.EARTH_GRID, handled by agents.earth_grid_agent.EarthGridAgent) executes automated parametric grounding grid sizing, mesh voltage calculation, step potential assessment, surface layer derating, and Ground Potential Rise (GPR) evaluations for high-voltage and medium-voltage substations.

    Because substation ground faults discharge hazardous single-phase-to-ground fault currents ($I_g \ge 1\text{ to }50\text{ kA}$) capable of causing lethal ventricular fibrillation, step/touch electrocution, and transfer-voltage equipment destruction: 1. Zero-Tolerance Safety Envelope: Every automated design calculation must strictly adhere to IEEE 80-2013 allowable touch and step voltage criteria using certified body weight limits (50 kg or 70 kg) and Dalziel ventricular fibrillation equations. 2. Fail-Closed Governance: The feature flag earth_grid is configured in api/feature_flags.py with enabled=False, rollout_percentage=0 (PRODUCTION-certified, NOT YET ROLLED OUT). No automated actuation, fabrication export, or production traffic may be admitted without site-specific field validation and Professional Engineer sign-off. 3. Zero-Guessing Constraint: Omission of essential engineering inputs (e.g. soil resistivity $\rho$, fault current $I_g$, clearing time $t_f$, or surface layer parameters) triggers immediate fail-closed termination (INSUFFICIENT_ENGINEERING_INPUT). Synthetic parameter substitution in production is strictly forbidden.

    2. Hazard Identification & Failure Mode Effects Analysis (FMEA)

    Hazard Primary Failure Mode Consequence Mitigation Strategy Severity Safety Boundary
    Touch Voltage Electrocution Excessive mesh potential ($E_m > E_{touch,allow}$) Lethal electric shock (ventricular fibrillation) to personnel touching grounded structures High surface layer resistivity ($\rho_s \ge 2500\,\Omega\cdot\text{m}$ crushed rock), grid density increase CRITICAL $E_m \le E_{touch,allow}$ (100% compliance mandatory)
    Step Voltage Electrocution High surface gradient ($E_s > E_{step,allow}$) Electrical shock across human stride during fault clearing Outer conductor grading, perimeter ground rods, surface gravel layer HIGH $E_s \le E_{step,allow}$ across 1.0 m stride
    Ground Potential Rise (GPR) High grid impedance under fault ($GPR = R_g \times I_g > 5\text{ kV}$) Transfer potential onto external neutrals, telecommunications, and control cables Deep driven rods, neutralizing transformers, remote isolation barriers CRITICAL Dedicated isolation if $GPR > 5000\text{ V}$
    Crushed Rock Washout / Degradation Erosion or mud contamination reducing $\rho_s$ Drastic reduction of allowable touch/step thresholds ($C_s \downarrow$) Periodic maintenance inspections, geotextile sub-layer, minimum 0.10–0.15 m thickness HIGH Minimum surface thickness $h_s \ge 0.10\text{ m}$
    Seasonal Soil Resistivity Fluctuations Frozen surface or summer soil desiccation ($\rho_{actual} > \rho_{design}$) Resistance rise, dangerous touch voltage inflation Seasonal Wenner 4-pin testing per IEEE 81, two-layer soil modeling HIGH Worst-case seasonal $\rho$ envelope

    3. Mandatory Operational Envelopes & Engineering Limits

    All automated study recommendations must adhere to the following certified engineering envelopes:

    1. Allowable Touch Voltage Envelope (Dalziel Criterion):
    2. For 70 kg body weight: $$E_{touch,70} = \left(1000 + 1.5\,\rho_s\,C_s\right) \times \frac{0.157}{\sqrt{t_s}}$$
    3. For 50 kg body weight: $$E_{touch,50} = \left(1000 + 1.5\,\rho_s\,C_s\right) \times \frac{0.116}{\sqrt{t_s}}$$
    4. Maximum allowable mesh utilization: $E_m / E_{touch,allow} \le 1.000$ (Design target: $\le 0.850$).
    5. Allowable Step Voltage Envelope:
    6. For 70 kg body weight: $$E_{step,70} = \left(1000 + 6.0\,\rho_s\,C_s\right) \times \frac{0.157}{\sqrt{t_s}}$$
    7. For 50 kg body weight: $$E_{step,50} = \left(1000 + 6.0\,\rho_s\,C_s\right) \times \frac{0.116}{\sqrt{t_s}}$$
    8. Maximum allowable step utilization: $E_s / E_{step,allow} \le 1.000$.
    9. Surface Layer Derating Factor ($C_s$):
    10. Calculated strictly via IEEE 80-2013 Eq. 27: $$C_s = 1 - \frac{0.91}{2 h_s + 0.09} \left(1 - \frac{\rho_s - \rho}{\rho_s + \rho}\right)$$
    11. Minimum surface layer resistivity $\rho_s$: Crushed rock baseline $\ge 2500\,\Omega\cdot\text{m}$.
    12. Minimum gravel layer thickness $h_s$: $\ge 0.10\text{ m}$ (standard: $0.15\text{ m}$).
    13. Grid Resistance & GPR Boundaries:
    14. Substation grid resistance target: $R_g \le 1.0\,\Omega$ for transmission/distribution substations; $\le 5.0\,\Omega$ for small distribution poles.
    15. Ground Potential Rise: $GPR = R_g \times I_g$. If $GPR > 5000\text{ V}$, special transfer-voltage mitigation and telecommunication isolation devices are mandatory.
    16. Fault Clearing Time Envelopes:
    17. Backup primary protection clearing time $t_f \ge 0.5\text{ s}$ must be used unless high-speed redundant breaker clearing ($t_f \le 0.2\text{ s}$) is certified by protection coordination study.

    4. Licensed Professional Engineer (PE) Review & Stamping Requirement

    Prior to deploying grounding grid installation schedules, trenching blueprints, or single-line grounding specifications: 1. Dual-Control Maker-Checker: - Automated grid designs require independent checker approval (api/approvals.py). Self-approval is blocked (MAKER_CHECKER_VIOLATION HTTP 403). 2. PE Digital Signature & Site Verification: - Field Wenner 4-pin soil test reports (IEEE 81) and final construction drawings must be signed and stamped by a licensed Professional Engineer.
