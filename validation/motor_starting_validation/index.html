    IEEE 399 Motor Starting Simulation Validation & Certification Report

    1. Study Overview & Baseline Standard

    • Study Type: motor_starting
    • Standard / Baseline: IEEE 399-1997 (IEEE Recommended Practice for Industrial and Commercial Power Systems Analysis — Brown Book, Chapters 9 & 10: Motor-Starting Studies) & NEMA MG 1.
    • Dynamic Formulation: Complete 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) integration of the induction machine electro-mechanical swing equation: $$\frac{d\omega}{dt} = \frac{1}{2H} (T_e(s, V_{bus}) - T_L(\omega))$$ coupled with dynamic bus voltage divider network algebraic equations: $$V_{bus}(s) = V_s \frac{Z_m(s)}{Z_s + Z_m(s)}$$ where $Z_m(s) = (R_s + R_r'/s) + j(X_s + X_r')$.
    • Target Metrics: Dynamic acceleration time ($t_{acc}$), minimum bus voltage dip ($V_{min}$), starting current envelope ($I_{start}(t)$), torque margin, and stall prevention.
    • Declared Tolerance Limit: $\le \pm 3.0\%$ acceleration time and $\le \pm 0.5\%$ minimum voltage dip vs analytical IEEE 399 reference solution.
    • Benchmark Status: VERIFIED_BENCHMARK (IEEE 399 Chapter 9/10 DOL and Star-Delta dynamic certification in engine/benchmarks/ieee_cases.py and scripts/run_ieee_benchmarks.py).
    • Lifecycle Status: PRODUCTION (Full deterministic compliance via engine/capability_registry.py:is_production_eligible()).

    Detailed Dynamic Case Parameters

    1. DOL Case (Direct-on-Line):
    2. Motor: $250\text{ kW}$, $400\text{ V}$, $4\text{-pole}$, $50\text{ Hz}$, $H = 0.5\text{ s}$, $I_{LRA}/I_{FLA} = 5.8$.
    3. Load: Quadratic fan load ($T_L = 0.20 + 0.80\omega^2$).
    4. Source Impedance: $Z_s = 0.01 + j0.04\text{ pu}$.

    5. Results: Motor smoothly accelerates to synchronous speed in $0.845\text{ s}$ with minimum voltage dip of $0.8184\text{ pu}$ ($18.16\%$ dip), well above stall limit ($0.70\text{ pu}$).

    6. Star-Delta Reduced-Voltage Case:

    7. Motor: Identical machine starting under star connection with reduced starting voltage ($1/\sqrt{3} \approx 0.577\text{ pu}$).
    8. Load: Quadratic profile with breakaway torque $0.15\text{ pu}$, rated load torque $0.60\text{ pu}$.
    9. Source Impedance: $Z_s = 0.01 + j0.04\text{ pu}$.
    10. Results: Accelerates to rated speed in $0.635\text{ s}$ with minimum voltage of $0.8944\text{ pu}$ ($10.56\%$ dip), reducing inrush by $30.6\%$ compared to DOL.
