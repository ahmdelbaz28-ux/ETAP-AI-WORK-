    IEEE 80-2013 Substation Grounding Grid Production Validation Report

    1. Study Overview & Baseline Standard

    • Study Type: earth_grid
    • Standards: IEEE 80-2013 (IEEE Guide for Safety in AC Substation Grounding, Section 14, Section 16 & Annex B) and IEEE 81-2012 (Guide for Measuring Earth Resistivity).
    • Mathematical Formulations: Rectangular grounding grid formulations (Sverak, Schwarz, and Laurent), mesh spacing factor ($K_m$), step geometric factor ($K_s$), irregularity factor ($K_i$), surface layer derating factor ($C_s$), tolerable touch/step voltage limits ($E_{touch}, E_{step}$), and Ground Potential Rise ($GPR$).
    • Target Safety Criteria: Mesh potential safety ($E_m \le E_{touch,allow}$), step potential safety ($E_s \le E_{step,allow}$), perimeter touch potential ($E_{touch,perim} \le E_{touch,allow}$), and ground resistance ($R_g \le 1.0\,\Omega$).
    • Zero-Guessing Constraint: Omission of engineering inputs triggers immediate fail-closed termination (AgentStatus.FAILED with error code INSUFFICIENT_ENGINEERING_INPUT). Synthetic parameter substitution in production is strictly forbidden.
    • Lifecycle & Governance: IN BETA ROLLOUT 100% — enabled=True, rollout 100, ENGINEER-TESTER PROGRAM, 2026-10-12, SHA feeb2e7a0, owner authorization reference (Owner-Ordered Full Beta Opening at 100%).

    [!WARNING] Beta-use warning: results are non-authoritative engineering assistance; any real-world design, purchase, or construction decision requires independent verification and a licensed PE stamp; report anomalies via the feedback channel with input data attached.

    3. Independent External Reference Benchmarks (Certifying)

    The certifying benchmark suite tests EarthGridAgent against published IEEE 80-2013 standard worked examples outside this repository: 1. IEEE 80-2013 Annex B Clause B.1 & Clause B.2 Primary Worked Example (GRID-IEEE80-70X70): 70 m × 70 m square substation grounding grid with 16 ground rods in uniform soil. 2. IEEE 80-2013 Section 14.3 & Annex B.4 Distribution Substation Grid (GRID-IEEE80-60X60): 60 m × 60 m distribution substation grounding grid with 20 ground rods, $1500\text{ A}$ fault current, and complete safety verification. 3. IEEE 80-2013 Section 16.5 & Section 16.6 Analytical Geometric Factors (GRID-IEEE80-GEOM-50X40): Closed-form mathematical verification of mesh factor $K_m$, step factor $K_s$, and irregularity factor $K_i$.
